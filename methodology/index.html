How the index is built

1. Infrared-only inclusion rule

The updater starts from the InHouse Wellness infrared sauna collection, then applies a second exclusion filter. A product is rejected if its title, product type or tags identify it as traditional, steam or hybrid. This intentionally excludes combination traditional/infrared cabins. Merely mentioning “traditional sauna-level heat” in descriptive copy does not exclude a pure infrared product.

2. Specifications

Prices and product metadata are refreshed from the retailer source. Dimensions, electrical requirements, capacity, infrared spectrum, EMF terminology and other fields are parsed when they appear in structured product data or product copy. Unknown values remain blank rather than being inferred.

3. EMF claims

We reproduce source terminology and measurement context. Low, Ultra Low and Near Zero are not treated as standardized regulatory categories. Measurements are only displayed as numerical claims when the source provides both the figure and, where available, measurement distance.

4. Retail relationship

InHouse Wellness is the featured retailer and receives outbound shopping links. Retailer status does not change physical-fit or electrical-fit scoring.

5. Update schedule

The GitHub Action is scheduled weekly and can also be run manually. The generated CSV and JSON files are published for inspection and reuse with attribution.
