What the labels mean here

“Low EMF,” “Ultra Low EMF” and “Near Zero EMF” are shown as product/manufacturer terminology. We do not treat them as standardized regulatory classes. Golden Designs, for example, describes Ultra Low EMF carbon panels as less than 3 mG at about 2 inches and Low EMF NIR elements around 5–10 mG at a similar distance; other brands may use different definitions or test methods.
